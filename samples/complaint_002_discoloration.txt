DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The pharmacy chain received their regular monthly shipment of Ibuprofen 400mg tablets on January 18, 2024. During stocking procedures, staff noticed that tablets in multiple bottles from batch IBU-20240110-B7 showed yellow-brown discoloration on the tablet coating.

The discoloration appears as irregular patches and spots on the white-coated tablets. Affected tablets are from 4 different bottles, all from the same batch. The remaining 6 bottles from the shipment appear normal.

Product has been stored in climate-controlled pharmacy conditions (68-72°F, 40-50% humidity) since receipt. No environmental exposure or mishandling incidents noted.

No tablets from affected bottles have been dispensed to customers. All affected product has been segregated and quarantined pending investigation results. Customer has photographed the defective tablets and can provide images upon request.

The pharmacy is concerned about:
• Product stability and efficacy
• Whether other bottles from this batch are affected
• Need for return/replacement of affected stock
• Whether this indicates a broader manufacturing issue

CUSTOMER EXPECTATIONS:
- Investigation findings within 10 business days
- Replacement product from unaffected batch
- Root cause explanation
- Assurance of corrective actions

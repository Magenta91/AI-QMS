Priority: HIGH
Initial Severity: Critical

Complaint Description:
Customer reports discovering what appears to be foreign particulate matter in two bottles of Amoxicillin 500mg capsules from batch AMX-2024-001-A. The foreign particles are described as small black specks, approximately 1-2mm in size, visible inside several capsules when held up to light.

The pharmacy received a shipment of 10 bottles (1000 capsules total) on January 12, 2024. During routine visual inspection before dispensing, the pharmacy technician noticed the contamination in 2 bottles. All 10 bottles have been quarantined and are being held for investigation.

No product from this batch has been dispensed to patients. The customer is requesting immediate investigation and batch disposition. They have segregated all affected material and are prepared to return samples for analysis.

Storage conditions have been maintained per label requirements (15-30°C, protected from moisture).

REGULATORY CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------------
- Potential for patient harm if contaminated product dispensed
- May require batch recall evaluation
- FDA reportable event consideration needed
- CAPA investigation required

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS REQUIRED
---------------------------
1. Quality investigation initiation
2. Batch hold and disposition review
3. Manufacturing records review
4. Retained sample examination
5. Risk assessment for other batches from same production run
6. Customer communication and product retrieval
